# Merrin

**Your personal intelligence. Private, on your device.**

Merrin is a private AI journal that helps you think, remember, and understand yourself over time. Talk or write about your day, and Merrin remembers what matters, connects your thoughts, and helps you notice patterns. Its core AI runs on your phone, not on a remote server.

## What Merrin does

- **Talk or type:** voice and text conversations that become journal entries automatically
- **Remember:** people, ideas, and decisions become a personal history you can search by meaning
- **Understand:** see recurring themes, open decisions, and how your views change over time
- **Perspectives:** respond as a Listener, Coach, Analyst, Future You, Devil's Advocate, or just talk freely

## Private by design

- Core AI runs on-device and works offline once the models are installed
- No account required, and your conversations are never used for advertising
- See, edit, or delete any memory, and back up to an encrypted file
